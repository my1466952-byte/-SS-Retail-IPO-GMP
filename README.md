## What Is SS Retail IPO GMP?

**SS Retail IPO GMP** refers to the Grey Market Premium associated with SS Retail's IPO. The grey market is an unofficial market where IPO shares may be traded before they are formally listed on a stock exchange.

For example, if the IPO's upper price band is ₹100 and the reported GMP is ₹20, the implied price based on GMP would be around ₹120. However, this does not guarantee that the stock will actually list at ₹120.

The actual listing price is determined by market demand and supply after the shares begin trading on the stock exchange.
